# FFXIV Housing for Non-Crafters: A Friendly Manual

*A fan-made guide, written by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by Square Enix. Contains no account data, no tracking, and no ads.*

Welcome! If you are level 15-ish, standing in your Free Company's yard, wondering
why everyone owns a house and a manderville-quality garden while you own exactly
one set of merchant-purchased gear — this manual is for you.

This guide is written for one specific kind of player:

- **The non-crafter.** You have never used a crafting log on purpose. You do not
  want to learn desynthesis. You just want a house (or an apartment — no pressure)
  that looks lived-in, your chocobo happy, your plants alive, and your glamour
  inventory under control.
- **The budget-curious.** Every gil figure here is real money to somebody. We do
  the buy-in math in plain numbers so nothing ambushes you.
- **The polite neighbour.** Because gardens, in FFXIV, are *social*. Section 4 is
  dedicated to not becoming the person the whole district talks about.

## How to use this guide

- Read pages 1 and 7 first if you already have the gil and just want to move.
- Read page 3 with a calculator (or don't — page 3 does the math for you).
- Every page ends with a **"For tomorrow"** box: one small next step. This is
  intentional. Housing is a marathon, and future-you needs breadcrumbs.

## Conventions used here

- **Gil** amounts are given in the millions ("3m gil") where possible, because
  pretending to recount seven digits at a time is where gil-based sadness begins.
- **Field Manual:** a shaded note marked *Hedge*: something we simply do not know
  to the gil. Check in game before you commit.
- **For tomorrow:** the small next step described above.
