Page 7 — Move-In Checklist Printables

A fan-made guide by Asdale Dynamics, LLC — unofficial, unaffiliated, ad-free, no account data, no sign-ups.

This page is two things: a movement plan for the hour after you get the keys, and a set of printable checklists built for the kind of night where you finally have the plot and immediately can't remember what you were going to do next.

Everything here is copy-paste friendly. Markdown tables, plain-text blocks — take them, print them, scribble on them.

1. The "got the keys" hour (a quick-start list)

Do these in order; each takes about a minute:

  1. Open the housing menu and read everything once. Menus hide features; the first hour is the cheapest time to find them.
  2. Locate your storage. Know where spare furniture goes before you start swapping things.
  3. Use the exterior fixtures. Door, outer walls, roof. A house with no door reads as a very committed cave.
  4. Find your permit (usually a ticket item in your inventory). Keep it. It is proof; it does nothing useful on the Market Board.
  5. Set the welcome message on your placard/entrance, if you want one. Short and warm beats clever.
  6. Light the place — interior lighting items first; mood does the rest.
  7. Take one screenshot. The "before" photo is the whole point of belonging somewhere — you'll want it in a year.

4. Printable checklist — the "guest visit" pass

Run this once before the first person sees the house. It's the checklist version of looking in the mirror before leaving:

GUEST-VISIT PASS — date/time of visit: ______

[ ] Entry path is clear (no furniture in the door)
[ ] Lights are actually on
[ ] A chair they can sit in
[ ] One topic-piece to look at (a garden, a shelf, a scene)
[ ] Welcome message current
[ ] Nothing in the yard that embarrasses you
[ ] Fresh water in the indoor planter (guests notice!)

Guest who visited: ______ (game name only — no real names in notes)
Something they liked: ______

5. A note about "printables"

Everything in this page is plain text on purpose, so it works if you:

  • print it on actual paper,
  • paste it into any notes app on any device,
  • share it as text in an FC forum thread.

There is no special app here, no account, no sync service. If you ever need to move the checklist to a different tool, it will survive the trip — that's the point of plain text.

For tomorrow

Do the session-1 checklist as far as you can tonight, and set a date (next weekend?) for session 2. Deadlines you set for yourself are the only housing deadlines that exist.
